## What is not claimed

- **35/40 fixture-verified is not 35/40 real user sessions.** Real-session coverage is **22/40**; fixture and constructed evidence are reported separately.
- Six canonical labels are **partial** (codex, cline, fx, kilo-code, roo, openhands) because of ATIF round-trip/lossy differences; they are not counted as full pass.
- Harnesses without installed local stores cannot be L3-verified on the build machine; L3 requires the target tool installed and a native load.
- Crush (FSL) and Zed (NOASSERTION/GPL mix) fixtures are not vendored; reader-only where applicable.
- Ad-hoc signed, not notarized; first launch may require right-click → Open.
