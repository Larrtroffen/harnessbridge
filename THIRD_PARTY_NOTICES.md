## 3. Samples, fixtures and captured sessions

- `samples/` contains sanitized session fixtures and captures used for tests, documentation and interop evidence. Provenance and batch notes live in `docs/batch2-samples.md`, `docs/formats/`, `docs/coverage-ledger.md`, `docs/atif-interop-protocol.md` and `docs/oss-trajectory-inventory.md`.
- Fixtures are derived from upstream open-source projects (e.g. sessionport, codeg, Harbor sample trajectories) or constructed from format documentation, as marked by the evidence levels (NATIVE / REAL-LOCAL / COLLECTED / FIXTURE / CONSTRUCTED).
- Samples are intended for interoperability development/testing. No API keys, credentials or personal account data are intentionally included; any real conversation content was reduced to excerpts/quotes needed to understand formats. If you spot sensitive content, please report it privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and it will be removed.
